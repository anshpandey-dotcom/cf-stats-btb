# Clapping Cat

AR app: places the clapping black cat in your room, anchored in 3D, and lets you shake its paw with the camera.

- Clap mode: tap the floor to place the cat. Pinch to resize. Tap again to move it.
- Handshake mode: the cat holds out its paw in front of the camera, reach your hand toward the camera.
- APK: built by the `Build Clapping Cat APK` workflow, download it from the repository Releases page or the workflow artifacts.
- `tools/` holds the scripts that removed the green screen and produced the clips and icons.
